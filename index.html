A reading primer

Read what you already speak.

Boli helps people who speak Odia, Telugu, or Punjabi learn to read their script. Learn the alphabet and the vowel signs a few at a time, each tied to a sound you already know, then start sounding out real words. It is free, works offline, keeps your progress on your device, and needs no account.

Pick a script to begin: Odia, Telugu, or Punjabi (Gurmukhi).
